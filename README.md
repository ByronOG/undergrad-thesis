# Automated Recognition of Tactics in Chess: Exploratory Analysis of the Role of Tactics in Gameplay

A thesis submitted in part fulfilment of the degree of **BSc. (Hons.) in Computer Science** at University College Dublin.

## Abstract
This project focuses on automating the identification of tactics, also commonly referred to as
*tactical motifs*, in chess games by leveraging advanced chess engines and external domain knowledge.
A tactical motif in chess refers to a specific pattern or combination of moves aimed at gaining
a tactical advantage, such as a material advantage or checkmate threat. By analysing millions
of games from the LiChess.org open database, this project aims to offer unique insights into the
game’s strategic intricacies. The development of *Tactical Recognition Algorithms* (TRAs) will
enable players to assess their tactical capabilities and identify areas for improvement. Through this
endeavour, the project seeks to contribute to the ongoing exploration of chess mastery and provide
valuable resources for players seeking to enhance their skills.
